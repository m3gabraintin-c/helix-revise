#### Reading: answer in English

**A1.** A summer Chinese camp (advertisement)
<!-- id: vce-chinese-sl34-ra1 -->
*Multi-part question · 7 marks*

**a.** Two weeks, from 8 to 20 January. *(1 mark)*

1. 共两个星期 means 'two weeks in total'; 一月八日至一月二十日 means from 8 January to 20 January. *(1 mark)*

Common mistake: Reading 一月 (January) as 'one month'. 一月 is the name of the month; a month-long period would be 一个月.

Topic: Reading: answer in English (vce-chinese-sl34-reading-english)

**b.** In the morning students study Chinese; in the afternoon they do cultural activities such as calligraphy, tai chi and making dumplings. *(2 marks)*

1. Mornings: Chinese lessons (上午学习中文). *(1 mark)*
2. Afternoons: cultural activities, for example calligraphy (书法), tai chi (太极拳) and making dumplings (包饺子). *(1 mark)*

Common mistake: Listing the activities without saying when they happen. The question asks about the day, so include morning and afternoon.

Topic: Reading: answer in English (vce-chinese-sl34-reading-english)

**c.** A performance, where students can sing Chinese songs or perform short plays. *(1 mark)*

1. 最后一天晚上有表演: there is a performance on the last evening; students can sing Chinese songs (唱中文歌) or act in short plays (表演小话剧). *(1 mark)*

Common mistake: Saying there is a party or a dinner. 表演 means a performance.

Topic: Reading: answer in English (vce-chinese-sl34-reading-english)

**d.** $1500 altogether ($750 each); the fee includes lunch and all materials. *(2 marks)*

1. Each sibling pays 800 − 50 = 750 dollars, so together they pay 1500 Australian dollars. *(1 mark)*
2. The fee includes lunch and all materials (包括午饭和所有材料). *(1 mark)*

Common mistake: Subtracting the $50 only once. 每人可以少付五十澳元 means each person pays $50 less.

Topic: Reading: answer in English (vce-chinese-sl34-reading-english)

**e.** Places are limited and are given on a first-come, first-served basis (enrolments close on 15 December). *(1 mark)*

1. 名额有限，先到先得: places are limited and go to whoever enrols first. *(1 mark)*

Common mistake: Giving only the closing date. The question asks why to enrol early: places are limited.

Topic: Reading: answer in English (vce-chinese-sl34-reading-english)

**A2.** My part-time job (blog post)
<!-- id: vce-chinese-sl34-ra2 -->
*Multi-part question · 9 marks*

**a.** On Saturdays and Sundays, for five hours each day. *(1 mark)*

1. 每个星期六和星期天…工作五个小时: every Saturday and Sunday, five hours each day. *(1 mark)*

Common mistake: Writing 'five hours a week'. The writer worked five hours on each of the two days.

Topic: Reading: answer in English (vce-chinese-sl34-reading-english)

**b.** There were many customers, and the writer often could not remember what they had ordered. *(2 marks)*

1. 客人很多: there were a lot of customers. *(1 mark)*
2. 我常常记不住他们点了什么: the writer often couldn't remember their orders. *(1 mark)*

Common mistake: Giving the reasons for the writer's later decision instead. Look at the sentence starting with 刚开始的时候 (at first).

Topic: Reading: answer in English (vce-chinese-sl34-reading-english)

**c.** The writer made coffee instead of the hot chocolate an elderly woman had ordered. She was not angry; she smiled and said it didn't matter and to take it slowly. *(2 marks)*

1. The mistake: an elderly woman's hot chocolate was made as a coffee (把…热巧克力做成了咖啡). *(1 mark)*
2. Her reaction: she wasn't angry and smiled, saying 'Never mind, young man, take your time' (没关系，年轻人，慢慢来). *(1 mark)*

Common mistake: Reversing the mistake (coffee made as hot chocolate). In a 把 sentence, 把 A 做成了 B means A was made into B.

Topic: Reading: answer in English (vce-chinese-sl34-reading-english)

**d.** Benefit: learning to communicate with different people (or learning that earning money is not easy). Disadvantage: less time for homework, and sometimes worse test results. *(2 marks)*

1. A benefit: learned to communicate with different people (跟不同的人沟通), or learned that earning money is hard (挣钱不容易). *(1 mark)*
2. A disadvantage: less time for homework (做作业的时间少了), and sometimes test results suffered (成绩受到了影响). *(1 mark)*

Common mistake: Giving two benefits or two disadvantages. The question asks for one of each.

Topic: Reading: answer in English (vce-chinese-sl34-reading-english)

**e.** To work only on Saturdays and use Sundays to revise and rest, because balancing study and work is very important. *(2 marks)*

1. Decision: work only on Saturdays, and use Sundays for revision and rest (只在星期六打工，星期天用来复习和休息). *(1 mark)*
2. Reason: the writer thinks balancing study and work is very important (学习和工作的平衡非常重要). *(1 mark)*

Common mistake: Saying the writer has quit the job. 只在星期六打工 means working on Saturdays only.

Topic: Reading: answer in English (vce-chinese-sl34-reading-english)

**A3.** Fewer plastic bags (article)
<!-- id: vce-chinese-sl34-ra3 -->
*Multi-part question · 8 marks*

**a.** About four billion. *(1 mark)*

1. 四十亿 = 40 × 100 million (亿) = 4 000 000 000, so about four billion. *(1 mark)*

Common mistake: Translating 四十亿 as 40 million or 400 million. 亿 is 100 million, so 四十亿 is four billion.

Topic: Reading: answer in English (vce-chinese-sl34-reading-english)

**b.** Many end up in the sea and seriously harm marine animals. *(1 mark)*

1. 很多最后进入了大海，对海洋动物造成很大的伤害: many end up in the ocean and cause great harm to sea animals. *(1 mark)*

Common mistake: Saying they pollute the air. The passage is about the sea (大海) and sea animals.

Topic: Reading: answer in English (vce-chinese-sl34-reading-english)

**c.** They can bring their own shopping bags, or pay a little for a reusable bag. *(2 marks)*

1. Bring their own bags (自己带购物袋). *(1 mark)*
2. Pay a small amount for a bag that can be used many times (花一点钱买一个可以多次使用的袋子). *(1 mark)*

Common mistake: Saying supermarkets still give free bags. 不再免费提供 means they no longer provide them free.

Topic: Reading: answer in English (vce-chinese-sl34-reading-english)

**d.** At first some found it inconvenient and often forgot their bags; after a few months most people got used to it, and now about 80% of customers bring their own bags. *(2 marks)*

1. At first: some people found it inconvenient and often forgot to bring bags. *(1 mark)*
2. Now: after a few months most people are used to it; about 80% (八成) of customers bring their own bags. *(1 mark)*

Common mistake: Reading 八成 as 'eight people'. 成 means tenths, so 八成 is 80%.

Topic: Reading: answer in English (vce-chinese-sl34-reading-english)

**e.** Any two: use fewer disposable chopsticks, use fewer plastic bottles, take public transport more often. *(2 marks)*

1. One suggestion, for example using fewer disposable chopsticks (少用一次性筷子). *(1 mark)*
2. A second suggestion, for example using fewer plastic bottles (塑料瓶) or taking public transport more (多坐公共交通). *(1 mark)*

Common mistake: Repeating 'use fewer plastic bags'. The question asks for other things, introduced by 除了…以外 (apart from).

Topic: Reading: answer in English (vce-chinese-sl34-reading-english)

### Writing in Chinese

#### Personal and imaginative writing

**A7.** Journal: my first day on exchange in China
<!-- id: vce-chinese-sl34-w1 -->
*Multi-part question · 10 marks*

**a.** A first-person journal entry of 200–300 characters, with a date, that describes the events of the first day and your feelings about them, and ends with a short reflection. *(10 marks)*

Sample response:

> 2026年7月5日　星期日　晴
>
> 今天是我到北京的第一天，我又兴奋又紧张。早上九点，飞机到了北京，我的寄宿家庭已经在机场等我了。王阿姨和她的女儿王静都很热情，一见面就帮我拿行李。
>
> 回家的路上，我看到了很多高楼和自行车，北京比我想象的大多了。中午，王阿姨做了一大桌菜，有宫保鸡丁、西红柿炒鸡蛋和饺子。我第一次用筷子夹饺子，饺子掉了好几次，大家都笑了，我也笑了。
>
> 下午，王静带我去了附近的公园。公园里有很多老人在打太极拳、跳广场舞，看起来非常开心。我也跟他们学了几个动作。
>
> 虽然今天有点儿累，而且有时候我听不懂别人说的话，但是我觉得这次交流一定会很有意思。希望在接下来的三个星期里，我的中文能有很大的进步。

1. Content: relevant, detailed events (arrival, host family, food, an outing) and feelings about them. *(4 marks)*
2. Text type: journal conventions (date and weather, first person, events in order, personal reflection at the end). *(2 marks)*
3. Language: accurate and varied vocabulary and grammar, for example linking words (虽然…但是…, 不但…而且…, 首先/其次/最后), complements and measure words used correctly, and few errors in characters. *(4 marks)*

Common mistake: Listing events with no feelings or reflection, or writing a letter (亲爱的…) instead of a journal entry.

**A8.** Story: the day I got lost
<!-- id: vce-chinese-sl34-w2 -->
*Multi-part question · 10 marks*

**a.** An imaginative narrative of 200–300 characters with a clear beginning (how you got lost), a problem and its solution, some dialogue or description, and an ending that says what you learned. *(10 marks)*

Sample response:

> 一次难忘的迷路经历
>
> 那是我第一次一个人去上海看朋友。下了火车以后，我的手机突然没电了，我也不记得朋友家的地址，只记得他家附近有一个很大的公园。
>
> 我站在火车站门口，心里非常着急。这时候，一位卖水果的老爷爷看见了我，问：“小伙子，你是不是迷路了？”我用不太流利的中文告诉他我的情况。他想了想，说：“这附近的大公园只有一个，我带你去吧！”
>
> 我们走了大概二十分钟，终于到了那个公园。真巧，我一眼就看见了在门口等我的朋友！原来他等了我很久，已经开始担心了。我想给老爷爷一点儿钱表示感谢，可是他笑着摆摆手就走了。
>
> 这次迷路的经历让我明白，出门以前一定要做好准备。更重要的是，我感受到了陌生人的温暖。这件事我永远也不会忘记。

1. Content: an engaging story with a setting, a problem, events that build to a solution, and descriptive detail or dialogue. *(4 marks)*
2. Text type: narrative structure (beginning, development, climax, ending) with a title and paragraphs, ending with a reflection. *(2 marks)*
3. Language: accurate and varied vocabulary and grammar, for example linking words (虽然…但是…, 不但…而且…, 首先/其次/最后), complements and measure words used correctly, and few errors in characters. *(4 marks)*

Common mistake: Telling the events as a list (first… then… then…) with no description or feelings, so the story has no tension.

#### Persuasive and informative writing

**A9.** Persuasive letter: keep Chinese at our school
<!-- id: vce-chinese-sl34-w3 -->
*Multi-part question · 10 marks*

**a.** A formal persuasive letter of 200–300 characters: a respectful greeting, a clear position, at least three reasons with examples, a polite request, and a formal closing. *(10 marks)*

Sample response:

> 尊敬的陈校长：
>
> 您好！我是十二年级的学生王大卫。听说学校打算明年取消中文课，我和很多同学都感到非常难过。我想借这个机会告诉您保留中文课的几个理由。
>
> 首先，中文是世界上说的人最多的语言之一。中国也是澳大利亚最重要的贸易伙伴之一，会说中文的人将来更容易找到好工作。
>
> 其次，学习中文不只是学习一门语言，也让我们了解中国的历史和文化。比如，上个学期我们参加了春节活动，学写书法、包饺子，同学们都觉得收获很大。
>
> 最后，我们学校有很多学生的家人会说中文，中文课让他们能更好地跟家人沟通。
>
> 总之，我认为中文课对学生的将来和学校的多元文化都很有帮助。希望您能认真考虑我们的意见，保留中文课。
>
> 此致　敬礼　学生 王大卫　2026年3月10日

1. Content: a clear position supported by several convincing reasons with examples (careers, culture, family and community). *(4 marks)*
2. Text type: formal letter conventions (尊敬的…, 您好, 此致 敬礼, signature and date) and persuasive structure (首先, 其次, 最后, 总之). *(2 marks)*
3. Language: accurate and varied vocabulary and grammar, for example linking words (虽然…但是…, 不但…而且…, 首先/其次/最后), complements and measure words used correctly, and few errors in characters. *(4 marks)*

Common mistake: Using 你 instead of 您 for a principal, or forgetting the formal closing (此致 敬礼). A persuasive letter to a principal must be polite and formal.

**A10.** Informative article: a club at our school
<!-- id: vce-chinese-sl34-w4 -->
*Multi-part question · 10 marks*

**a.** An informative article of 200–300 characters with a title, an engaging opening, clear facts (when, where, who, what happens), the benefits, and how to join. *(10 marks)*

Sample response:

> 我们学校的机器人俱乐部
>

>
> 在俱乐部里，同学们分成小组，一起设计、制作机器人，并给机器人编程。老师会教大家基本的编程知识，所以就算你以前没有经验也没关系。每年八月，俱乐部还会参加全州的机器人比赛。去年，我们的队伍得了第二名！
>
> 参加机器人俱乐部不但可以学到很多科学知识，而且能提高团队合作和解决问题的能力。如果你想报名，请在学校网站上填写报名表，或者直接去二号实验室找李老师。

1. Content: accurate, well-organised information about the activity (time, place, members, what happens, achievements, benefits, how to join). *(4 marks)*
2. Text type: article conventions (a title, an opening that speaks to the reader, one main idea per paragraph, a practical ending). *(2 marks)*
3. Language: accurate and varied vocabulary and grammar, for example linking words (虽然…但是…, 不但…而且…, 首先/其次/最后), complements and measure words used correctly, and few errors in characters. *(4 marks)*

Common mistake: Writing about your own feelings only, like a diary. An informative article must give the reader clear facts they can use.

### Oral exam: conversation (GC)

#### Conversation: family, friends and interests

**A11.** 我家有四口人：爸爸、妈妈、哥哥和我。爸爸是工程师，妈妈是护士。我哥哥今年二十岁，在墨尔本大学学医。周末我们一家人常常一起去海边散步，有时候也一起做饭。
<!-- id: vce-chinese-sl34-gc1 -->

Explanation: Useful structures: 我家有…口人; 在…学…; 常常 / 有时候. In the exam the assessors will follow up on what you say, so add a detail you are happy to talk about. Possible follow-up: 你跟哥哥的关系怎么样？
